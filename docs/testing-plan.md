# Testing Plan – Solar Wireless Charger

## 1. Experimental Overview

The initial experimental phase focuses on understanding and testing wireless electrical energy transfer using two copper coils.

An initial receiving coil was created using copper wire and connected to a 5V battery as part of the preliminary setup.

The next objective is to develop a suitable transmitting coil capable of generating a changing magnetic field and transferring energy to the receiving coil through electromagnetic induction.

## 2. Initial Experiment – Receiving Coil

### Setup
- Copper wire wound into a coil.
- 5V battery used in the initial experimental setup.
- Receiving coil prepared for wireless energy transfer experiments.

### Objective
To explore the receiving-side arrangement and understand how electrical energy can be induced through a magnetic field generated by a separate coil.

### Current Status
Initial coil construction completed.

Further testing is required to establish the induced voltage, current and actual power transfer.

## 3. Next Experiment – Transmitting Coil

### Objective
Develop a suitable primary (transmitting) coil to generate a changing magnetic field capable of inducing voltage in the secondary (receiving) coil.

### Planned Investigation
- Transmitting coil winding configuration.
- Number of coil turns.
- Coil diameter.
- Wire thickness.
- Distance between the two coils.
- Alignment between the coils.
- Operating frequency.
- Transmitted and received voltage.

### Expected Outcome
Establish a working inductive wireless energy transfer arrangement.

## 4. Testing Parameters

The following parameters will be measured during subsequent experiments:

| Parameter | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Input voltage | Voltage supplied to transmitter |
| Input current | Current drawn by transmitter |
| Induced voltage | Voltage generated in receiver |
| Output current | Current delivered to load |
| Coil temperature | Thermal performance |
| Coil separation | Effect of distance |
| Alignment | Effect of coil positioning |
| Transfer efficiency | Energy transfer performance |

## 6. Safety Considerations

- Avoid directly connecting an experimental coil to a battery without suitable current limiting.
- Use an appropriate driver circuit for the transmitting coil.
- Monitor coil and component temperatures.
- Avoid short circuits and excessive current.
- Use suitable battery protection and charging circuitry.
